# CNC Routers

Welcome to the **My Machines CNC** documentation hub. Bring accessible, industrial-grade subtractive manufacturing directly to your desktop or workshop. 

Our open-source CNC routers are engineered for exceptional rigidity and precision. Whether you are milling custom electronic circuit boards (PCBs), routing hardwoods, or cutting aluminum parts, our comprehensive assembly manuals and open-source software guides will help you set up a robust and reliable machining ecosystem.
