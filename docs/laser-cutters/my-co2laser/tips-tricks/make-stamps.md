# Creating Custom Rubber Stamps with Neon Studio

Making high-quality rubber stamps with your **Neon** is a straightforward process once you understand how to create the necessary relief. This guide covers the design setup, mirroring, and material-specific tips.

---

## 1. The "Negative" Design Strategy

To create a stamp, the laser must engrave the background and leave the letters untouched (raised). In **Neon Studio**, we achieve this by using a black background and white text.

1.  **Create the Base:** Draw a black rectangle that will serve as the outer boundary of your stamp. Move this to your engraving layer.
2.  **Add Text:** Create your text or logo. Change the fill color to **White**.
3.  **The Logic:** The **Neon** only fires where it sees black pixels. By placing white text over a black rectangle, the laser will remove the background and leave the white areas at the original height of the rubber.

---

## 2. Alignment and Precision

For a professional look, your text must be perfectly centered within the stamp base.

1.  Select your text, hold **Shift**, and then select the black rectangle.
2.  Navigate to **Object > Align and Distribute**.
3.  Click the buttons for **Center on Vertical Axis** and **Center on Horizontal Axis**.

---

## 3. Mirroring: The Golden Rule

Remember: for a stamp to read correctly when pressed onto paper, the design on the rubber must be a **mirror image**.

1.  Select your entire design (Rectangle + Text).
2.  Click the **Horizontal Flip (Mirror)** icon in the top toolbar. Your text will now appear backward—this is correct!

## 4. Material Tips: Working with Stamp Rubber

Not all rubbers are the same. Here are the professional standards for the **Neon**:

* **Engraving Side:** Most laserable rubbers have a matte side and a glossy side. Usually, you should engrave on the **matte side** (confirm with your supplier).
* **High Resolution:** For crisp text, change the engraving resolution to **High** in the top-right settings of **Neon Studio**.
* **Hardness & Parameters:** * Softer rubbers may use **Speed 100 / Power 20**.
    * Harder, high-quality "low-odor" rubbers may require **Speed 50 / Power 100**.
    * **Note:** Harder rubbers might not cut all the way through in one pass. It is often easier to finish the outer cut with a utility knife once the deep engraving is done.

---

!!! success "Pro Tip: Clean Up"
    Laser engraving rubber produces a fine gray dust. After the job is done, clean your stamp with a soft toothbrush and slightly soapy water to remove any residue from the crevices before mounting it to a handle.
