# I have air bubbles in the laser tube

A common issue when starting the water cooling system is the appearance of air bubbles at the ends of the laser tube. These bubbles are highly detrimental to the performance and lifespan of your **Due Flow** and must be removed before operation.

## Removal Techniques
If you notice bubbles in the tube, follow these steps in order. Be persistent, as it may take a few tries for the air to clear completely.

### 1. Power Cycle
Turn off the machine entirely and wait at least **10 seconds** before switching it back on. This simple reset can sometimes flush the air out through the initial water pressure.

### 2. Manual Hose Pressure
Gently squeeze and release the water hose several times to encourage the bubble to move. This is most effective when done on the **left side** of the hose.

  ![](../images/construcao-light.png#only-light){ width="400" }

### 3. Tilt the Machine
Carefully lift the side of the machine opposite to where the bubble is trapped. Gravity often helps the air pocket travel toward the outlet.

### 4. Agitate the Tube Axis
Firmly move the tube axis back and forth. The goal is to "shake" the bubble loose from the internal walls of the tube. While you should be careful not to damage the hardware, this movement requires some intensity to be effective.

---

!!! warning "Still stuck?"
    If the air bubbles persist despite these efforts, please stop operation and contact our **Technical Support**. We will analyze your case closely to ensure your equipment remains safe!
