# Other Maker Projects

Welcome to the **My Machines Extra Projects** hub. A true open-source workshop never stops evolving. This section is dedicated to the experimental hardware, custom add-ons, and peripheral tools designed to elevate your entire digital fabrication ecosystem.

From pushing the boundaries of multi-color 3D printing to embracing the **circular economy** by recycling plastics into usable filament, these projects empower you to take complete control of your manufacturing pipeline.
