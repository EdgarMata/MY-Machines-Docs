# Local Network Integration (Wi-Fi Infrastructure Mode)

This method allows you to integrate the **Neon** into your existing workplace Wi-Fi. By doing so, the machine becomes a node in your local network, similar to a wireless printer.

---

## Network Integration Overview

Connecting the **Neon** to your local network offers distinct operational advantages but requires a stable infrastructure.

* **Key Benefits:** Allows simultaneous internet access and machine control. It also simplifies multi-user environments, enabling different computers on the same network to interface with the hardware.
* **Technical Requirement:** To initiate this setup, you must first establish a temporary connection via the **Neon Access Point** or a physical Ethernet cable.
* **Signal Stability:** Performance is directly linked to your router's signal strength. For high-bandwidth tasks, ensure the **Neon** is positioned within optimal range of the Wi-Fi router to avoid packet loss or latency.

---

## Configuration Process: Step-by-Step

Follow these steps within **Due Studio** to bridge the machine to your local network:

### 1. Access System Settings
On the Due Studio Control Bar, locate and click the **Settings** (gear) icon to open the configuration panel.

### 4. Finalizing Connection
If you used the temporary **Neon** Wi-Fi to perform this setup, you may now switch your computer back to your workplace Wi-Fi. Verify the "Connection Status" icon in the software; once it turns green, the **Neon** is successfully integrated into your local ecosystem.

---

!!! info "Connection Troubleshooting"
    Inconsistent signal or incorrect credentials can prevent successful integration. If you experience dropouts, consult our guide on **Local Network Troubleshooting** or consider a wired Ethernet connection for maximum reliability.
