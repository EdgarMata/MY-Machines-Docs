## Pro Tips for Best Results

* **Use High Resolution:** Always opt for high-resolution settings. This significantly improves the finish and detail of engravings on curved surfaces.
* **Locking the Motors:** Objects with uneven weight (such as a mug with a handle) may rotate unexpectedly. It is best practice to lock the motors via software after positioning your object.
* **Power Parameters:** If you are working with a new material, start with low power settings. It is safer to run a second pass than to risk damaging the object.
* **Glass Engraving:** Ensure the surface is thoroughly cleaned with alcohol. Since glass is sensitive to temperature changes, using low power is crucial to prevent cracking.

* **Handling Irregular Shapes:** For standard cylinders, use the diameter. For tapered objects (like traditional cups), measure the **circumference** of the specific engraving area to avoid distortion.
