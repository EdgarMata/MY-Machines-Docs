# Mirror Cleaning Guide

Your **Due Flow** is equipped with a set of mirrors responsible for reflecting and directing the laser beam. For the machine to operate perfectly, it is fundamental that these mirrors are kept spotless!

!!! danger "Attention: Safety First"
    Before starting any cleaning procedure, ensure the machine is **disconnected from the power supply**.

---

##  Cleaning Frequency

This procedure should be performed **weekly**. However, depending on the materials you use, more frequent care may be required. 

* **MDF Users:** This material releases smoke that quickly creates a resin layer on the mirrors. If you work extensively with MDF, we recommend **daily cleaning**.
* **Performance Tip:** If your machine seems to be losing power, it might just be dirty mirrors!

---

##  Required Materials

To clean the mirrors effectively, you will need:
* Cotton swabs (Q-tips)
* Neutral detergent (mild soap)
* Warm water

##  Locating the Mirrors

1.  **Central Carriage Mirror:** Located inside the moving black housing on the X-axis (laser head).
2.  **Side Mirrors:** These two mirrors are located on the left side of the machine, fixed to the motion gantry.

##  Cleaning Procedure

Follow these steps carefully to avoid scratching the reflective surface:

1.  **Apply Detergent:** Place a small drop of neutral detergent on a cotton swab and gently apply it to the mirror using circular motions.
2.  **Rinse with Warm Water:** Dip a fresh swab in warm water and gently clean the mirror in a circular motion to remove the soap. (You can also pre-mix the water and detergent).
3.  **Repeat if Necessary:** Continue the process until all residue is gone.
4.  **Dry Thoroughly:** Use a dry cotton swab to remove any excess moisture. **Ensure there are no streaks or water spots left behind!**

Repeat this process for all mirrors in the machine.
