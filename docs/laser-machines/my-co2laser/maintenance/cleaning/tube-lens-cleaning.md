# Laser Tube Lens Cleaning

In this guide, we will cover how to clean the laser tube lens. This specific lens is a vital part of your machine's optical path, ensuring the beam exits the tube with maximum power.

---

##  Locating the Lens

The laser tube lens is slightly hidden at the output end of the glass tube. You don't need to remove the side panels to reach it; a bent cotton swab is usually enough to access the area from behind the tube.

##  Cleaning Frequency

* **Standard Maintenance:** This procedure should be performed **monthly**.
* **High-Intensity MDF Cutting:** If you cut MDF frequently, we suggest performing this cleaning every **15 days**. 
* **Performance Tip:** If you notice a sudden drop in laser power, a dirty tube lens is often the culprit!

!!! danger "Safety Warning"
    Always perform this cleaning with the machine **disconnected from the power outlet** to avoid any risk of electrical discharge or accidental laser firing.

---

##  Required Materials

* Cotton swabs (Q-tips)
* Warm water mixed with a drop of neutral detergent
* Isopropyl alcohol (99% purity recommended)

---

##  Cleaning Procedure

Since the lens is hard to see directly, you must be thorough and ensure the swab reaches every corner:

1.  **Initial Wash:** Dip a cotton swab into the warm water and detergent mixture. Apply it to the lens using circular and zigzag motions.
2.  **Thoroughness:** Because you cannot see the surface clearly, repeat the process a few times to ensure full coverage.
3.  **Alcohol Finish:** Use a fresh cotton swab dipped in **isopropyl alcohol**. Repeat the same circular and zigzag movements to remove any remaining residue or soap streaks.
4.  **Inspection:** Ensure the lens is dry and clear before turning the machine back on.
