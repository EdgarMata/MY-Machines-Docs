# Reduce Vibration

## Guide

Reducing vibration can improve print quality. Here are some tips:

1. **Secure the Printer**: Place the printer on a stable surface. A heavy, solid table can help reduce vibrations.
2. **Use Dampers**: Install vibration dampers on the motors. These can absorb vibrations and reduce noise.
3. **Adjust Acceleration and Jerk**: Lower these settings to reduce sudden movements. This can help minimize vibrations and improve print quality.
4. **Print Slower**: Slower print speeds can reduce vibrations. Start with a speed of 40-60mm/s and adjust as needed.
5. **Check for Loose Parts**: Ensure all parts are securely fastened. Loose parts can cause vibrations and affect print quality.

### Additional Tips

- **Use Anti-Vibration Pads**: Place anti-vibration pads under the printer to absorb vibrations.
- **Optimize Print Path**: Use slicing software to optimize the print path and reduce unnecessary movements.
- **Regular Maintenance**: Perform regular maintenance on your printer to ensure all parts are in good condition and properly tightened.

### Example Procedure

1. **Secure the Printer**: Place the printer on a stable, heavy table.
2. **Install Dampers**: Install vibration dampers on the stepper motors.
3. **Adjust Acceleration and Jerk**: Lower the acceleration and jerk settings in your printer's firmware or slicing software.
4. **Print Slower**: Reduce the print speed to 40-60mm/s.
5. **Check for Loose Parts**: Regularly check and tighten all screws and bolts on the printer.

By following these steps, you can minimize vibration and improve the overall quality of your prints.
