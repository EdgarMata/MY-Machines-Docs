The Bill of Materials (BOM) identifies the parts required to build the **My-Cloner Rev A**.

This section provides a human-readable view of the BOM to help you identify, source and prepare the required components before starting the build.

!!! info "Authoritative BOM"
    The **My-Cloner Rev A BOM V2** is the authoritative Bill of Materials for this hardware revision.

    The pages in this section provide a simplified reference for component identification and sourcing.

## How the Documentation Fits Together

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **BOM** | Identify **what parts are required** for the build. |
| [**Assembly**](../assembly/01_introduction.md) | Learn **how the mechanical parts are assembled**. |
| [**Wiring & Electronics**](../wiring/index.md) | Learn **how the electrical components are connected**. |
| [**Downloads**](../downloads/index.md) | Obtain the **BOM, STL, CAD, firmware and other project files**. |

A typical build workflow is:

1. Review the BOM and source the required components.
2. Print the required 3D printed parts.
3. Follow the Assembly Guide.
4. Complete the wiring using the Wiring & Electronics documentation.
5. Continue with the initial setup and calibration procedures.
