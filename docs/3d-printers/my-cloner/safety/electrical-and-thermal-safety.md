# Electrical and Thermal Safety

A 3D printer combines high temperatures and electricity. Understanding and respecting these two factors is essential for safe operation.

!!! danger "Severe Burn Hazard"
    The printer's operating temperatures can cause severe and instantaneous burns.
    * **Do not touch the nozzle** during heating or operation. Its temperature can range from **210°C to 300°C**.
    * **Do not touch the heatbed** while it is hot. Its temperature can exceed **100°C**.
    * Always wait for both components to cool down completely before removing parts or performing maintenance.

!!! warning "Electrical Hazard"
    The printer is an electrical device. To prevent shock or damage:
    * Never operate the printer if you suspect a damaged cable or an exposed electrical component.
    * Do not operate the printer in humid environments or if it has been exposed to liquids.
    * In case of any anomalous behavior (smoke, burning smell), immediately disconnect the printer from the power outlet.
