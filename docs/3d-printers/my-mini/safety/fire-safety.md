# Fire Safety

While 3D printers are generally safe, they are complex electro-mechanical devices that operate at high temperatures. It is crucial to be aware of the potential fire risks and take preventative measures.

---

## Key Fire Safety Measures

### 1. **Never Leave the Printer Unattended**
- This is the single most important rule of 3D printing. A running 3D printer should be treated like an open flame or a running stove. 
- If you must leave the room, ensure it is only for short periods. For long prints, consider using a remote monitoring solution.

### 2. **Install a Smoke Detector**
- Install a smoke detector directly above or near the 3D printer. This will provide an early warning in the event of a fire.

### 3. **Keep a Fire Extinguisher Nearby**
- Have a fire extinguisher readily accessible near the printer.
- **Recommended type:** An **ABC dry chemical** extinguisher is suitable for most types of fires you might encounter (Class A: combustibles, Class B: flammable liquids, Class C: electrical fires). A **Class C** or **CO2** extinguisher is also a good choice as it is specifically for electrical fires and will not damage electronics.

### 4. **Ensure Quality Components and Wiring**
- **Good wiring practices:** Ensure all electrical connections are secure and insulated. Avoid a "rat's nest" of wires, which can be a hazard.
- **Quality components:** Use a reputable power supply and high-quality electronic components. Cheap, uncertified components are a common cause of 3D printer fires.

### 5. **Firmware Safety Features**
- **Thermal Runaway Protection:** Ensure this feature is enabled in your printer's firmware. It is designed to shut down the printer if it detects a fault in the heating system, which is a primary cause of fires.

---

## In Case of Fire

1.  **Cut the power:** If it is safe to do so, disconnect the power to the printer at the wall outlet or circuit breaker.
2.  **Use the fire extinguisher:** Use the appropriate fire extinguisher to put out the fire.
3.  **Evacuate:** If the fire is too large to handle, evacuate the area immediately and call your local emergency services.

By taking these precautions seriously, you can operate your **MY-Mini 3D Printer** with confidence and peace of mind.
