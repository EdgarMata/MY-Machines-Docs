# Safety

Safety is fundamental when assembling, operating, and maintaining your MY-Mini-3D-Printer. Follow these recommendations, inspired by best practices from the My-Cloner-3D-Printer and Prusa Mini.

---

## Safe Use
- **Read the Manual:** Always consult the user manual before operating the printer.
- **Ventilation:** Use the printer in a well-ventilated area to avoid inhaling fumes.
- **Supervision:** Never leave the printer running unattended.
- **Fire Extinguisher:** Always have a fire extinguisher nearby.
- **PPE:** Wear safety glasses and gloves when handling hot parts or tools.

## Electrical Safety
- **Regular Inspection:** Periodically check cables and electrical connections.
- **Grounding:** Ensure the printer is properly grounded.
- **Avoid Water:** Keep the printer away from water and moisture.
- **Power Off Before Handling:** Unplug before any maintenance.
- **Do Not Overload:** Do not use uncertified power strips or extension cords.

## Thermal Safety
- **Hot Surfaces:** The nozzle and bed can reach high temperatures. Wait for them to cool before touching.
- **Thermal Protection:** Make sure the firmware has thermal runaway protection enabled.
- **Ventilation:** Print in a ventilated area to dissipate heat.

## Power Supply Handling
- **Check Voltage:** Confirm the power supply is set to the correct voltage for your region.
- **Visual Inspection:** Look for signs of wear or damage before each use.
- **Unplug Before Intervention:** Never work on the power supply while plugged in.

## Emergency Procedures
- **Fire:** Use an appropriate extinguisher for electrical fires.
- **Electric Shock:** In case of shock, cut the power and seek medical assistance.
- **Burns:** Cool the affected area with running water and seek medical help if necessary.

## Prusa Mini Tips
- Always follow the manufacturer's recommendations for upgrades and modifications.
- Keep children and pets away from the print area.

> Safety first: a well-maintained printer is a safe printer!
