# Cleaning the Print Bed

A clean print bed is essential for good first layer adhesion, which is the foundation of a successful 3D print. This guide provides instructions on how to clean the print bed of your **MY-Mini 3D Printer**.

---

## Materials Needed
- Isopropyl alcohol (IPA) 90% or higher
- Lint-free cloths or paper towels
- Warm water
- Dish soap (optional, for deep cleaning)
- Plastic scraper (for removing stubborn prints)

---

## When to Clean the Print Bed
- **Before every print:** A quick wipe with IPA is recommended.
- **When you see fingerprints or dust:** Oils from your skin can prevent filament from sticking.
- **When switching between different filament types:** Some filaments can leave residues that affect the adhesion of others.
- **If you are experiencing adhesion issues:** A thorough cleaning can often resolve these problems.

---

## Cleaning Procedures

### Routine Cleaning (Before Every Print)

1.  **Ensure the bed is cool:** For safety, always clean a cool print bed.
2.  **Wipe with IPA:** Lightly dampen a lint-free cloth with isopropyl alcohol (90%+).
3.  **Clean the surface:** Wipe the entire print surface to remove any dust or oils.
4.  **Let it dry:** Allow the IPA to evaporate completely before starting a print.

### Deep Cleaning (For Adhesion Issues or Contamination)

If you are still having adhesion problems after a routine cleaning, a deep clean may be necessary.

1.  **Remove the print surface (if removable):** If your printer has a removable magnetic or glass bed, it can be easier to clean it at a sink.
2.  **Wash with soap and water:**
    *   Use warm water and a small amount of dish soap.
    *   With a soft cloth or sponge, gently wash the print surface. This will break down any stubborn grease or sugar residues from filaments like PLA.
    *   Rinse the bed thoroughly with warm water to remove all soap residue.
3.  **Dry completely:** Dry the print surface with a clean, lint-free cloth. Make sure it is completely dry before reinstalling it.
4.  **Final IPA wipe:** Give the bed one last wipe with IPA to ensure it is free of any remaining contaminants.

---

## Tips for a Clean Print Bed

- **Avoid touching the print surface:** The oils from your fingers can significantly impact print adhesion.
- **Use a plastic scraper:** If you need to remove a stubborn print, use a plastic scraper to avoid scratching the print surface.
- **Keep the printer environment clean:** A dusty environment will lead to a dusty print bed.

By following these cleaning procedures, you will ensure the best possible first layer adhesion and improve the reliability of your **MY-Mini 3D Printer**.
