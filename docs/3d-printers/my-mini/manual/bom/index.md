# Bill of Materials (BOM)

Below is the detailed list of components needed to assemble the MY-Mini-3D-Printer. This printer was designed to be robust, easy to assemble, and inspired by the open-source philosophy of the Prusa Mini, with improvements in electronics, structure, and ease of maintenance.

## How to use this list
- Each section details the components, quantities, and recommended purchase links.
- Also check the assembly and upgrade tips throughout the documentation.
- For questions or suggestions, join the My Machines community.

### Project Highlights
- Aluminum extrusion frame for maximum stability.
- Modern, silent electronics (MKS SGEN_L V2.0 + TMC2209).
- Magnetic heated bed with removable PEI sheet.
- Removable touch display and USB/Ethernet connectivity.
- Compatible with Prusa Mini slicer profiles.

- [Electronics](electronics.md) — Board, drivers, sensors, display, power supply, etc.
- [Mechanical Parts](mecanical-parts.md) — Frame, screws, bearings, belts, etc.
- [3D Printed Parts](printed-parts.md) — All required printed parts.

---

## Prusa Mini Inspiration
The MY-Mini-3D-Printer follows the Prusa Mini philosophy: simplicity, modularity, and quality. Many parts and solutions are compatible with or inspired by the Prusa Mini, making upgrades and maintenance easier.

> Also see the [Prusa Mini Inspiration](../../about/prusa-mini-inspiration.md) section for more on similarities and differences.
