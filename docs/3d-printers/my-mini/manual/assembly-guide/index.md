# Assembly Guide

This guide will help you assemble your MY-Mini-3D-Printer step by step, with practical tips and inspiration from the Prusa Mini.

## 1. Frame and Preparation
- Assemble the aluminum frame, ensuring all profiles are well aligned and tightened.
- Use a square to ensure accuracy.
- Install brackets and corner pieces as shown in the drawings.

## 2. Installing Motors and Motion System
- Attach the Nema 17 motors to their mounts.
- Install the GT2 pulleys and belts, adjusting the tension to avoid slack.
- Mount the linear bearings (LM8UU and LM10LUU) and check for smooth axis movement.
- Install the lead screw and couple it to the Z motor.

## 3. Heated Bed and Bed Assembly
- Attach the heated bed base to the frame.
- Install the steel sheet with PEI for easy part removal.
- Connect the thermistor and bed power cables.

## 4. Electronics Installation
- Mount the MKS SGEN_L V2.0 board and organize cables with zip ties.
- Install the TMC2209 drivers and configure for silent mode (UART).
- Connect motors, endstops, fans, and sensors according to the wiring diagram.
- Install the MKS H43 touch display and connect it to the mainboard.

## 5. Wiring and Organization
- Use cable chains or spirals to organize cables.
- Ensure there are no loose cables or cables in contact with moving parts.

## 6. Initial Tests
- Power on the printer and check if all components are recognized.
- Test axis movement, sensors, and heating.
- Perform bed leveling (mesh bed leveling).

## Prusa Mini Tips
- The Prusa Mini uses a modular assembly system, making upgrades and maintenance easier. Follow this philosophy for future improvements.
- Check Prusa Mini videos and manuals for assembly and cable management tips.
