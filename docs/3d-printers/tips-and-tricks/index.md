# Tips and Tricks

Discover advanced tips, upgrades, and tricks to get the most out of your MY-Mini-3D-Printer, inspired by the Prusa Mini community.

## Recommended Upgrades
- Install an automatic bed leveling sensor (BLTouch or P.I.N.D.A.).
- Add LED lighting to the print area.
- Use printed holders for cable management.
- Try nozzles of different diameters for specific applications.

## Popular Mods
- Higher flow part cooling fan for better surface finish.
- Filament holder with bearings.
- Adaptation for flexible filaments (TPU).

## Printing Tricks
- Use "brim" or "raft" for small parts.
- Test different infill patterns for strength.
- Print calibration parts regularly.

## Prusa Mini Tips
- Explore Prusa's GitHub and forums for compatible mods.
- Share your solutions and improvements in the My Machines community.

> Innovation is continuous: customize and optimize your printer to suit your needs!
