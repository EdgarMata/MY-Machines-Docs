# Replacing the Nozzle

Replacing a nozzle is a common maintenance task, required when a nozzle is hopelessly clogged, worn out from abrasive filaments, or when you wish to change to a different orifice size.

The procedure for a reactive replacement is identical to a preventive one. Please refer to the detailed, step-by-step guide in the Preventive Maintenance section for full instructions.
