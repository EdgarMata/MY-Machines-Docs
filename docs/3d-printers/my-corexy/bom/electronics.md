# Electronics BOM

This document lists all the electronic components required for the MY-CoreXY-3D-Printer.

## Non-Printed Parts

This section details the non-printed electronic components.

| Part Number | Description | Quantity | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| EL-0001 | Controller Board | 1 | e.g., SKR Pro, Duet 3, or similar. |
| EL-0002 | Stepper Motor Driver | 5 | TMC2209 or similar. |
| EL-0003 | Endstop Switch | 3 | Mechanical or optical switches. |
| EL-0004 | Thermistor | 1 | 100K NTC thermistor for the hotend. |
| EL-0005 | Heater Cartridge | 1 | 24V 40W heater cartridge. |
| EL-0006 | Cooling Fan | 2 | 4010 or 5015 fans for part cooling and hotend. |
| EL-0007 | Wiring Kit | 1 | Includes various connectors and wires. |
