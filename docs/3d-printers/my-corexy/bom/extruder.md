# Extruder BOM

This document lists all the parts required to assemble the extruder for the MY-CoreXY-3D-Printer.

## Non-Printed Parts

This section details the non-printed components for the extruder.

| Part Number | Description | Quantity | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| EX-0001 | Extruder Motor | 1 | NEMA17 pancake stepper motor. |
| EX-0002 | Hotend | 1 | e.g., E3D V6, Phaetus Dragonfly, or similar. |
| EX-0003 | Extruder Gear Kit | 1 | Bondtech or similar dual-drive gears. |
| EX-0004 | PTFE Tube | 1 | For guiding the filament into the hotend. |
| EX-0005 | Pneumatic Fitting | 2 | To connect the PTFE tube. |

## Printed Parts

This section lists all the printed parts for the extruder. All files are provided in the correct orientation for printing.

### Printing Instructions
The following table shows the recommended print settings.

| Perimeters | Layer Height | Infill | Material | Supports |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 0.2mm - 0.3mm | 25% | PETG, ASA or ABS | As needed |
