# User Manual

<div class="announcement">
  <p>📢 Welcome to the official assembly and user guide for the MY-CoreXY 3D Printer!</p>
</div>

The MY-CoreXY 3D Printer manual is a comprehensive guide designed to assist you from the very first step to your final calibration. This manual is divided into several main sections to provide detailed, step-by-step information on all aspects of your new printer.

- The **Getting Started** section covers all the preliminary steps, including essential safety warnings, part printing guidelines, and a list of required tools.
- The **Assembly Guide** provides detailed visual instructions for the entire mechanical build, from the frame to the final enclosure panels.
- The **Electronics & Wiring** section offers a deep dive into mounting and connecting all electronic components safely and correctly.
- The **Initial Setup & First Use** guide covers the software installation, Klipper configuration, and the exciting process of turning on your printer and completing your first print.
- The **Bill of Materials (BOM)** provides a complete checklist of all the parts you will need.

Each section is designed to be user-friendly, with clear instructions and helpful tips to ensure a smooth and successful build experience. Explore the links below to dive into each section.
