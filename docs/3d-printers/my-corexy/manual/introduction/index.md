# Welcome to the Assembly Manual

Welcome to the assembly guide for your CoreXY printer! This project is designed with the philosophy of creating a high-performance 3D printer, inspired by top-tier designs like the Voron. Our motto is: **"We build space shuttles with gardening tools, so anyone can have a space shuttle of their own."**

This manual will guide you step-by-step through the entire mechanical and electrical assembly process.

!!! danger "Safety First - Read Before You Begin"
    You are about to assemble a robot in your home. This machine, if not handled with proper care, can **maim, burn, and electrocute you**. Your safety is your own responsibility. Please read and understand the following warnings:

    * **Mains Voltage:** You will be working with lethal mains voltage (110V/230V). Always disconnect the printer from the wall outlet before touching any electrical components. High-voltage wiring should only be performed by qualified personnel, following local standards.
    * **Hot Components:** The hotend and print bed reach temperatures that can cause severe burns. Handle with care.
    * **Moving Parts:** The motors in this machine are powerful. Keep your fingers, hair, and loose clothing away from moving belts, pulleys, and gantries to prevent injury.
    * **Fire Safety:** Never leave the printer running unattended. Keep an appropriate fire extinguisher nearby.
